# Focus timer sounds

Taken from [ryOS](https://github.com/ryokun6/ryos) (`public/sounds/`),
which is AGPL-3.0. The sounds themselves are not ryOS's own work: they are
recordings of old system sounds, so their copyright stays with the original
makers, and they are bundled here for a fan-made desktop toy, like the
characters.

- `AlertIndigo.mp3`: the classic Mac OS "Indigo" alert sound (Apple).
- `EmailMailSent.mp3`, `EmailNoMail.mp3`: the Mail "sent" and "no new
  mail" sounds (Apple).
- `Boot.wav`: the Macintosh Quadra startup chime (Apple). ryOS ships it as
  `Boot.mp3`, but it is a WAV file.
- `MSNNudge.mp3`: the MSN Messenger nudge (Microsoft).
